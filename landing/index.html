It will not spam anyone: each asset goes out at most once per customer, three media messages per customer per day, and the agent is told what it already sent, so it refers back instead of resending.

Why does this matter?

Today, an attachment with no caption is silently dropped: the agent never runs, and the conversation stalls until a human notices. That is the number one reason media threads need a person.

Set it up in three steps

About five minutes, no code. Your own instance walks you through the rest.

  1. Get your free copy running. Make a free Render account, then deploy. It generates its own keys and sets itself up, about two minutes.
    1. Create Render account 2. Then deploy
  2. Connect your account. Open your new page, paste your Assistable key, a read-only token from your CRM, and a free AI key. Pick your assistant. The tool is created for you.
  3. Your agent understands media. Send a voice note with no caption to your number. It reads it and replies, live on the page, then you are done.
Deploy not going through on Render?

Make the Render account first (button 1), then come back and click deploy. If you still land on a blank dashboard, in Render click New, then Blueprint, then paste this under "Public Git Repository": github.com/Hari487-coder/assistable-media-bridge

Free plan note: Render's free tier sleeps after fifteen idle minutes and wipes its disk on redeploys, which is fine for trying it. For real customer traffic, the Starter tier keeps its data and stays awake.

Questions

What do I need before I start?
Two accounts plus one free key: Render, which hosts your instance, the Assistable account you already have, and a Gemini API key on Google's free tier, which covers voice, images and PDFs. No card.
What access does it need?
From Assistable, a v3 API key so it can create the reader tool and reply. From your CRM, a read-only integration token for conversations only. It never touches contacts, calls or billing, and every key is validated live before it is saved.
Where does my data go?
Nowhere but your own instance. Voice notes and photos are read in memory and never stored to disk or logs, and your keys are encrypted at rest on the copy you deployed.
Which channels work?
Any channel your CRM handles: WhatsApp, SMS and MMS, Facebook, Instagram. One setup covers all of them, because they all arrive the same way.
What does it cost to run?
You bring your own AI key, so there is no bill from us and nothing shared centrally. Free tiers cover a pilot comfortably.
